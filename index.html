Current work

And Action!

A generative AI production environment for film. I took it from concept to a working platform as sole product owner and engineer, using AI-assisted development.

The production problem

A generated clip is only part of a shot. A team also needs to know how it was made, return to the setup, compare revisions and pass the work to the next person.

What I built

A shared canvas that keeps the workflow graph and parameters behind each take, connected to versioned assets, team review and timeline editing.

Where it stands

Live with its first invited users. Model integrations, real-time collaboration and role-based permissions are in place. I can walk through how a take is reopened, revised and handed off.

  • Return to the sourceReopen the workflow and parameters behind a generated take.
  • Choose the right modelSwap generation engines without rebuilding the workflow.
  • Review in contextKeep asset versions, team feedback and editorial together.
  • Control accessGive collaborators and clients the permissions their work needs.

And Action! is in private beta. Patent pending.
